**Quiz - Think DSP ch.1-2: 54/80 (6.75/10).** Fundamentals solid; the aliasing/Nyquist cluster is the weak spot.
- Q1 period/frequency/Hz: 9. Q2 spectral decomposition + DFT/spectrum: 8. Q3 fundamental/dominant/harmonics: 6 (harmonics are integer multiples of the FUNDAMENTAL, not the dominant; multiples go up). Q4 phase offset: 9 (ear is phase-insensitive). Q5 wave/frame/sample: 8. Q6 aliasing: 5. Q7 Nyquist/folding freq: 5. Q8 applied Nyquist: 4.
- Aliasing = a high-frequency component undersampled masquerades as a LOWER frequency (folds to |fs - f|).
- Nyquist / folding frequency = HALF the sampling RATE (fs/2), not the interval between samples. To avoid aliasing, sample at MORE THAN TWICE the highest frequency (fs > 2*f_max).
- Worked example: fs=44,100 -> Nyquist 22,050 Hz; a real 30,000 Hz tone is ABOVE Nyquist so it aliases to |44100-30000| = 14,100 Hz. Real ADCs low-pass filter before sampling to prevent this.

Mends to carry: (1) aliasing/Nyquist = fs/2, test frequencies above/below it (numbers, not "interval"); (2) harmonics = integer multiples of the fundamental. Language to watch: "twice" vs "half" - I keep flipping these, so anchor with a concrete number every time.
