## Dependency swamp (and the fix)
RAGAS 0.4.3 wouldn't import under my LangChain 1.x install. Two eager imports inside RAGAS pointed at paths that moved in LangChain 1.x:
- `langchain_community.chat_models.vertexai` (Vertex moved to langchain-google-vertexai in 1.x).
- `langchain_openai` wanting `ContextOverflowError` from a newer langchain-core than I had.
Root cause: RAGAS 0.4.3 targets the pre-1.0 (0.3.x) LangChain line; my env was 1.x. Shimming one import just revealed the next (whack-a-mole). Clean fix: fresh Python 3.12 venv + pin the whole LangChain family <1.0 in one resolve. (Also learned: Python 3.14 had no wheels for half the stack, which was fueling the mess.)

## Controlled retrieval study (fixed 15-question eval set, committed)
First mistake caught: I compared hybrid-on-15Q against an old dense-on-4Q number - two variables at once, invalid. Rebuilt a fixed 15-question set and compared properly (same set, same judge, one knob).

| config | context_recall | context_precision | faithfulness | answer_relevancy |
|--------|----------------|-------------------|--------------|------------------|
| dense, k=8, chunk=1000 (baseline) | 0.533 | 0.375 | 0.500 | 0.359 |
| hybrid (BM25 + dense, 50/50) | 0.400 | 0.286 | 0.467 | 0.308 |
| dense, chunk=500, k=8 | 0.267 | 0.236 | 0.333 | 0.173 |

**Findings (both experiments HURT - and that is a legitimate, explained result):**
- Hybrid worse on this corpus: BM25 keyword matching on narrative prose surfaces chunks that share common words but not meaning; at 0.5 weight those noisy hits displace good semantic matches in the RRF fusion. BM25 helps keyword-heavy/technical corpora, not literary text.
- Smaller chunks worse: with TOP_K fixed at 8, halving chunk size roughly halves the total text retrieved -> coverage drops -> recall craters. To shrink chunks I'd have to raise k to keep coverage constant. Small chunks also fragment scene-spanning answers.
- Winner: the dense k=8 chunk=1000 baseline. Reverted serving to it.

**Meta-lessons:**
- Controlled comparison is everything: fixed eval set + change ONE knob, or the delta means nothing (the hybrid-vs-4Q mistake).
- Negative results, explained, are worth documenting - shows rigor.
- The local gemma4:e4b judge is a rough grader and n=15 is smallish, so absolute numbers are directional; trust clear moves (dense > hybrid), not decimals.
- Real next levers if revisited (from Anthropic Contextual Retrieval): contextual chunks (LLM prepends chunk context before embedding), reranking, a stronger embedding model. Heavier; parked - retrieval micro-tuning has hit diminishing returns vs building the LangGraph agent.
